# Acts 2 and 3 - level beats for Levie (same shape as act1_beats.md)

## Act 2 - Borrowed Light (paid)
### Floor 5 - Homes
- Mood: dwarf family homes, hearths cold and black, cradles, kitchens. Drip zones begin.
- Light: tide-glow (cold blue-green) from puddles; no fire anywhere except Wren's.
- Story: Orrun's house. Elda's letters (`f5_elda_letter`) - she opposed the pact. Her hearth was the last fire given.
- Must-have: Orrun's house (big hearth, two chairs), a children's room, a locked study with the 2nd letter.
- Monsters: tide spawn (debut), drowned dwarf families (sad, slow), eels in drip zones.
- Exit hook: a path of glowing moss leads down. Wren: "Someone planted these to light the way."

### Floor 6 - Sunken Garden (the calm floor)
- Mood: a huge glass dome of held water, fish swimming overhead, phosphor plants, stone benches.
- Light: soft green-gold glow from plants and the dome. Beautiful and wrong.
- Story: Orrun's gift to Elda. Her grave (`f6_elda_grave`, flag `elda_ring`). Mural of the pact being sealed here.
- Must-have: the grave, a mural wall, a bench where the party can rest (full heal, idle barks), few monsters.
- Exit hook: a crack in the dome lets the singing in - and it's in Ilsevar's language.

### Floor 7 - Pact Grotto
- Mood: natural cave, water everywhere, glass-water walls you can walk past, offering bowls of ash.
- Light: almost none. Lantern gutters a lot (heavy drip zones).
- Story: the Tide courts Ilsevar (`f7_grotto`, flag `ilsevar_listened`). If listened: Ilsevar can part glass water for the rest of the game.
- Monsters: tide spawn, bog horrors, a tide-shaped mini-boss.
- Exit hook: warmth below. Brannoc: "I smell coal. Real coal."

### Floor 8 - Cold Forges (Act 2 finale)
- Mood: great forge halls, anvils, bellows, chains, ovens. All cold.
- Light: Wren RELIGHTS forges one by one (interact) - each lit forge permanently lights its room and dries drip zones.
- Story: Hessa (`f8_hessa`, flag `hessa_trusted`). If `saved_tam`, Tam finds a dry shortcut.
- Cliffhanger: Orrun is alive; the Heartforge is almost out. [Act 2 end]

## Act 3 - The Heartforge
### Floor 9 - Great Engine
- Mood: giant bellows breathing, pistons, brass pipes, glowing runes. Loud.
- Light: engine glow pulses with the bellows (bright, dim, bright).
- Story: you SEE how the keep breathes. Brannoc's mechanism barks peak here.
- Puzzles: timing walks through pistons, valve levers that redirect air (open/close rooms).
- Monsters: engine golems, drowned engineers.

### Floor 10 - Thane's Vault
- Mood: treasure halls, statues of thanes, a crown room.
- Story: the seal (`f10_seal`, flag `brannoc_claimed`). If claimed, drowned dwarves in Act 3 bow instead of fighting.
- Monsters: vault guardians, the honour guard.

### Floor 11 - Drowned Throne
- Mood: throne hall half full of still water, Orrun on the throne, body part water.
- Story: `f11_throne`. Ring/Dural/rage paths. Then a PLAYABLE FLASHBACK: the party plays as Orrun the night the keep sank, carrying fire to the Tide, Elda's letter in hand (short, scripted, 1 small map).
- Exit: Orrun dissolves into the floor and flows down to the Heartforge.

### Floor 12 - Heartforge
- Mood: the last fire. A forge the size of a house, guttering. Water walls on all sides.
- Light: only the Heartforge and Wren's lantern.
- Story: Orrun fight (skippable if ring + captain), then `f12_final` -> endings.
- If `saved_tam`: Tam warns when the Tide moves. If not: drowned Tam fights for the Tide.
- Ending B adds a timed climb up the air shafts (reuse the escape-run system).
