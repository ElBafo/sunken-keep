# NPCs

## Tam Reedly (F3 heard, F4 met, F8/F12 if saved)
- Mags's younger brother, 19. Went into the keep last spring chasing giant frogs for coin.
- The Tide has been "keeping" him half-drowned in a flooded cell: alive, gills of weed on his neck,
  talks in drips. It uses him as bait for warm-blooded visitors.
- Voice: cheerful, confused, too calm. "Oh hey Maggie. Is it supper?"
- If saved: weak at first, then he can *hear the water* like Ilsevar. In F8 he finds a dry route; in F12
  he warns when the Tide moves. Required for the best ending.
- If left: Mags goes cold for a while (fewer jokes, sharper barks). In F12 Tam appears drowned, on the
  Tide's side. Mags gets one line: "You should've let me carry him."

## Captain Dural Ashmantle (F4, F11 if spared)
- Captain of Orrun's honour guard. Drowned but lucid, bound by his oath to guard the cells.
- Voice: formal, tired, iron. "State your clan."
- Respects Brannoc if he names himself Stonevow. Can be fought or parleyed (`captain_spared`).
- If spared: in F11 he stands at the throne and tells Orrun the truth: "My Thane. It is over."

## Hessa Coalbright (F8)
- Master smith of the Cold Forges. Drowned, but the only one of the drowned who kept her mind,
  because she kept a coal burning in her chest all sixty years. Smoke leaks from her mouth when she talks.
- Voice: blunt, practical, funny. Flirts with Brannoc shamelessly. "Shame about the beard. Mine's better."
- Explains the engine, the air, the pact. Reveals Orrun is alive.
- Asks Wren for a spark to light her anvil (`hessa_trusted`); if given, the lantern dims for the floor,
  but she forges the party a Heartforge weapon for F12.

## Thane Orrun Stonevow (F5 told, F11 met, F12 boss)
- Made the pact to save his clan when the keep began to sink. Gave the Tide fire floor by floor. Gave it
  his wife Elda's hearth last - she died of cold. He never forgave himself and never stopped feeding it.
- Now: kept alive by the Tide on the Drowned Throne, body half water. He thinks he's still saving them.
- Voice: grand, broken, repeating himself. "One flame given. One floor spared."
- Boss in F12 unless the party convinces him (needs `captain_spared` or `elda_ring`).

## Elda Stonevow (F5, F6 - dead)
- Orrun's wife. Gardener. Never seen alive; known from her letters in the Homes and her grave in the Garden.
- Her letters are the heart of Act 2: she argued against the pact. "Let the water come, love. We'll go up."
- Her ring (`elda_ring`) is the key to reaching Orrun.

## The Hollow Tide (voice from F4)
- The swamp's hunger. Not evil, just endless. Speaks through water: every puddle, drip, and the drowned.
- Text shown in a cold blue-green, lowercase, no speaker portrait.
- Voice: soft, plural, patient. "warm ones. you brought light. how kind."

## Hobb Rushwick (F2)
- Halfling frog-catcher from Mags's swamp town, owes her six coppers. Pinned under a fallen dwarven shelf in the stores nook.
- Voice: loud, cheerful, aggrieved. Knows Tam went down after the big frogs and that "the water was singing to him".
- Freed (`frogcatcher_freed`): gives a red potion and warns which lever floods his nook.
- Drowned (`frogcatcher_drowned`): the drain lever floods the nook first. Teaches early that actions have costs.
