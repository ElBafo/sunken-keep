# Endings

Final choice at the Heartforge (F12), after the Orrun fight or parley. Options shown depend on flags.

## A. Rekindled (always available)
Wren pours her lantern into the Heartforge. The forge roars; the engine breathes; the Tide is pushed back
for another age. Orrun, freed, crumbles to ash at peace. Wren's lantern is out - she says she'll make
another. The party climbs out with daylight on their faces. Bittersweet good.
- Last line - Mags: "Right. Who's carrying the gold?" Brannoc: "Who said there's gold?"

## A+. The Keeper's Peace (best: `saved_tam` AND `brannoc_claimed`)
Brannoc, wearing the Thane's seal, commands the drowned to tend the forge forever - they are dwarves
again, at work. Tam reads the water and finds the Tide's real hunger: it was never fire, it was company.
The party makes a new pact: a fire is lit for the Tide every year, a feast. No one is given up.
- Last line - Brannoc: "Stonevow Keep. Under new management."

## B. Let It Drown (always available)
The party refuses the pact. The Heartforge gutters out; the engine stops; the water comes. Escape run UP
the air shafts (timed sequence). Orrun stays and drowns with his keep, finally. The swamp swallows Stonevow.
- Last line - Ilsevar: "The swamp keeps what it's given. Let's not give it us."

## C. The Offering (always available, but costly)
Give a party member to the Tide: the keep is saved for good and the Tide sleeps. The player picks who.
Each hero has a farewell line. The survivors leave in silence.
- Brannoc: "Tell them a Stonevow went down with the keep. Like he should have."
- Wren: "Keep the lantern lit for me. Or don't. I'll know."
- Ilsevar: "I always heard it. Now I'll answer."
- Mags: "If you spend my share on anything sensible, I'll haunt you."

## C-. The Hollow King (darkest: `ilsevar_listened`)
Ilsevar steps forward before the player can choose. He offers himself and BECOMES the Tide. The keep is
saved, the water rises in a gentle wave, and his voice joins the drips. Post-credits: years later, a new
party enters, and the Tide greets them in Ilsevar's voice: "warm ones. you brought light. how kind."
